Dialect, pronunciation & lesson notes

The currently available beginner course focuses on Eastern Sureth (Assyrian Neo-Aramaic). Family and village dialects vary. Roman spelling follows common spoken forms; it is a reading aid rather than a letter-by-letter transliteration. The full Syriac spelling may retain letters that are not pronounced. “Sounds like” cues are approximate English guides, not audio or exact stress notation.

Some everyday alternatives, including kha / khad, tre / tren, ati, akhni and ane, are accepted in typing practice. The model “to be” endings distinguish male and female speakers and addressees. Vowel marks distinguish endings such as iwen and iwan; keep them if you answer in Syriac.

Language reference checks: Sami Hormis · Ma’alta, Assyrian Circles · Eastern beginners’ guide, Eastern Assyrian phrases, present “to be” forms, and Sureth dictionary.
